01 / THE IDEA

What NoteMind does

It is a learning prototype, not a finished research platform.

⌕

Find relevant passages

Notes are split into chunks. Embeddings help compare a question with those chunks and select likely matches.

✦

Answer from your material

The selected passages are sent with the question to Gemini, with instructions to stay grounded in the supplied context.

▤

Review what you learn

Generate summaries, multiple-choice quizzes, and flashcards to turn uploaded material into revision practice.

02 / UNDER THE HOOD

How the pieces connect

Simplified architecture
01BrowserReads files, splits text, stores chunks and embeddings in IndexedDB, and ranks chunks with cosine similarity.
→
02Flask APIRuns the app endpoints and keeps the Gemini API key on the server rather than in the page.
→
03Gemini APICreates embeddings and generates answers, summaries, quizzes, and flashcards from provided context.
03 / CURRENT WORKFLOW

From upload to answer

  1. Add a sourceTXT, Markdown, PDF, or a supported image.
  2. Index the textText is split into overlapping chunks and embedded.
  3. Ask a questionThe browser ranks chunks and sends the best matches to the API.
  4. Check the evidenceOpen source snippets to compare the answer with the original extracted text.

Before you upload

Please review these limitations before adding files to NoteMind.

  1. Learning prototype: NoteMind is an educational project, not a commercial-grade research system or a replacement for Google NotebookLM.
  2. AI can be incorrect: summaries, answers, quizzes and flashcards may contain mistakes or omit important details. Verify important information against your original materials.
  3. Sources are not a guarantee: source snippets show text selected by the retrieval process. They do not prove that an answer is complete or correct, and relevant passages may be missed.
  4. External AI processing: extracted note text and questions may be sent to Google's Gemini API to create embeddings and generate responses. Images and scanned PDFs may be sent for OCR. Google's service and data-handling terms apply to that processing.
  5. Local browser storage: notes, embeddings and chats are stored in this browser on this device. Clearing browser site data may remove them. The application is not a secure document vault.
  6. Basic access controls: this prototype does not provide user accounts, private workspaces or application-level rate limiting. Do not assume the public demo is suitable for confidential or high-volume use.
  7. Extraction limitations: scanned PDFs, handwriting, low-resolution images, complex tables and unusual formatting may be read incorrectly or not at all.
  8. Supported file types: TXT, Markdown (.md), PDF, PNG, JPG/JPEG and WEBP. Only upload files you have permission to use.
Do not upload passwords, API keys, identity documents, financial or medical records, private work documents, or other sensitive/confidential material.
