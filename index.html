How NoteMind works

NoteMind answers questions from your own notes and shows the exact text it used. This pattern is called retrieval-augmented generation (RAG).

System architecture

Your browserSplits notes into sections, stores their embeddings, finds the closest sections to your question
→
Flask on VercelTwo endpoints, /api/embed and /api/answer. Holds the API key and stores nothing
→
Gemini APITurns text into embeddings and writes the answer from the sections it is given

What happens when you ask

  1. You add a note. The browser cuts it into sections of about 800 characters with a small overlap.
  2. The server asks Gemini to turn each section into an embedding, a list of numbers that captures its meaning.
  3. The embeddings stay in your browser memory. Refreshing the page clears them.
  4. Your question gets an embedding too. The browser compares it with every section using cosine similarity and picks the closest five.
  5. Those five sections and your question go to Gemini, which is told to answer only from them, or say it does not know.
  6. The answer appears with source pills. Click a pill to read the original text.

Privacy

Nothing is saved on the server. Your note text does go to Google's Gemini API to create embeddings and answers, so do not upload passwords, ID numbers or personal documents.
